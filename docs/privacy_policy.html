NRA Video Uniquifier — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 30, 2026 • Effective date: October 30, 2026

1. Summary

NRA Video Uniquifier is an offline, on-device video utility. We do not collect, transmit, share, or store any personal information on external servers.

2. Data Collection

We collect NO personal information. Specifically:

  • No analytics, telemetry, or crash reporting services.
  • No advertising identifiers or tracking SDKs.
  • No precise or coarse location data.
  • No user account or login registration required.
  • No external network access required for application functionality.

3. On-Device Media Processing

All video modifications and transformations occur strictly locally on your device:

  • Source videos remain in your device storage and are never uploaded.
  • Processed videos are saved directly to your device's Movies/Uniquifier directory.
  • Temporary working cache files are automatically purged when the application starts.
  • Media files are never transmitted anywhere unless you explicitly initiate an external share intent via the system share sheet.

4. Application Permissions

  • Storage Access (Read/Write): Required solely to read source videos selected by the user and save transcoded videos to user-accessible storage.
  • Notifications: Required to display real-time render progress and render completion alerts (optional, user-controllable).
  • Foreground Service: Enables background encoding persistence so that video renders continue uninterrupted when the app is minimized.

5. Third-Party Services

NRA Video Uniquifier does not integrate any third-party ad networks, tracking analytics, or external cloud storage providers. All dependencies operate offline.

6. Children's Privacy

The application is not directed toward children under 13 years of age. Because no data is collected, no information from any individual of any age is ever acquired or stored.

7. Policy Updates

Any material changes to this policy will be clearly communicated via the Google Play Store update release notes and updated on this page.

8. Contact Information

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@novrira.id
