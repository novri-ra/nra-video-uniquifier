# NRA Video Uniquifier — Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** 2026-10-30  
**Effective:** 2026-10-30

## Summary
NRA Video Uniquifier processes videos entirely on your device. We do not collect, transmit, or store any personal data on external servers.

## Data Collection
We collect NO personal information. Specifically:
- No analytics or crash reporting services
- No advertising identifiers
- No location data
- No account registration required
- No network access required for app functionality

## Data Processing
All video processing occurs on-device:
- Source videos remain on your device
- Processed videos are saved to your device's Movies/Uniquifier folder
- Temporary cache files are purged on app startup
- Files are only shared externally if you explicitly tap "Share"

## Permissions
- Storage (read/write): to access videos for processing and save outputs
- Notifications: to show progress and completion alerts (optional)
- Foreground service: to continue processing when app is backgrounded

## Third Parties
No third-party services are used. No SDKs send data externally.

## Children's Privacy
The app is not directed at children under 13. No data is collected from any user regardless of age.

## Changes
Any material changes will be announced in the app's release notes.
